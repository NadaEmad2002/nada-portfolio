01

SiaCore AI

Supply Chain Risk Intelligence · Team graduation project

A multi-agent AI system for electronics supply-chain risk intelligence. It combines a company's internal supply-chain data with external market and industry signals to show which components and suppliers are at risk, why, and what to do about it.

How the system works

  • Specialized agents for data acquisition, news intelligence, and Product Change Notification (PCN) monitoring continuously detect events affecting components and suppliers.
  • A multi-dimensional risk framework covers distributor, manufacturer, stock and inventory, alternatives, compliance, and other supply-chain risks.
  • Risk signals from all agents are integrated to link component-level risks with external events.
  • An explainability and recommendation layer turns detected risks into understandable, evidence-backed mitigation actions.

My contribution

  • Developed a web scraping agent that collected data from 1,000+ distributors worldwide.
  • Cleaned, filtered, and processed the collected data.
  • Integrated it into the project's shared database, contributing to the data foundation behind the risk intelligence system.

Technologies

  • Web Scraping
  • Data Processing
  • PostgreSQL
